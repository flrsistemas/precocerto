Preço Certo

Know the real cost of every recipe, and the price that pays you for your work.

Get it on Google Play How it works Privacy policy

Android app for bakers and confectioners.

What it does

You list the ingredients and the time a recipe takes. Preço Certo adds up the cost of the ingredients, the gas and electricity, and your own labour, and shows how much each unit really costs. You pick the profit margin you want and the app gives you the selling price.

Real cost per recipe

Ingredients, indirect costs and your working time, all in one number per unit.

One selling price

Choose your margin and get a single selling price, rounded up to the next 10 cents. The profit shown is calculated on that exact price.

Recipe parts

Sort the ingredients into dough, filling, seasoning, decoration and packaging.

Backup file

Save your recipes in an encrypted file, share it, and restore it on a new phone.

How it works

  1. Set what your hour is worth

    Open the menu and choose My Business. Fill in the income you want each month, your hours per day and your days per month. The app shows your hourly rate and cost per minute.

    Here you also set the defaults for new recipes (overheads for gas, electricity and wear, and your profit margin). Tap Save settings. Your business name goes on the quotes you send.

  2. Add your pantry items

    Go to Pantry and tap New item. Enter the name, the price you paid for the whole package, and the package amount and unit. The app works out the cost per gram, millilitre or unit on its own.

    Packaging counts too: boxes and liners by the piece, ribbon and film by the metre.

  3. Build a recipe

    Tap New recipe. Give it a name, add the pantry items with the amount you use, and sort them into parts such as dough and filling. Write the method, the hands-on time, and how much the recipe yields.

  4. Read the price

    At the bottom of the recipe you see the total cost and the cost per unit. Move the profit margin slider and the app gives you the selling price, rounded up to the next 10 cents, with the profit per unit. If rounding raises the margin, a note tells you the margin it ended up with. Tap Save recipe.

  5. Send a quote or a kitchen sheet PRO

    Open a recipe and tap Create a quote. Choose to charge by Unit or by Whole recipe, set the quantity and who it is for, and tap Create PDF. The quote shows the price and never your costs or your margin.

    Kitchen sheet makes a PDF with every ingredient by part and the method, with no prices.

  6. Back up your recipes

    In My Business, the backup card (Back up now) saves everything in one encrypted file that you can share. Restore it from the same card (Restore backup) when you change phones.

  • Add your pantry items first, then build the recipe: the recipe needs them to work out the cost.
  • Update an item's price when you buy it again, and the recipes that use it update on their own.
  • The app uses your phone's language and region, so money and numbers look the way you are used to.
