Privacy Policy

Preço Certo app · FLR Sistemas · Effective September 17, 2026

Preço Certo collects no personal data.

There is no server, no sign-up, no login and no account. Your recipes, your ingredients and your prices are stored only on your phone, and we have no way of seeing them.

Who we are

Preço Certo is developed and maintained by FLR Sistemas. For any question about this policy, write to flrsistemas@gmail.com.

The data you create in the app

As you use Preço Certo you enter information about your business: recipe names, ingredients, packaging, purchase prices, preparation time, profit margin and preparation notes.

All of that content is written only to the app's private storage, inside your device. It is never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. We cannot access it, we cannot recover it, and we keep no copy of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for a sign-up, login, email, phone number or any identifier.
  • It uses no analytics, usage statistics or tracking of any kind.
  • It shows no advertising and uses no advertising identifier.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or files.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone — because it holds none.

Android backup

Android offers an automatic backup service that copies app data to the user's own Google Drive. If that service is turned on for your device, the file containing your recipes will be copied to your Google Drive and restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone.

It is worth being precise about what this means: that copy lives in your Google account, under Google's own policies, and exists so that you do not lose your work. We have no access to it.

You can turn the backup off, or delete an existing copy, in your device settings under Google › Backup. Your Pro licence is not included in that backup.

Buying the Pro version

The Pro version is a one-time purchase, processed entirely by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google under its own privacy policy — we never see, receive or store card numbers, addresses or banking information.

The app stores only a local record on your device that the purchase was made, so that it can unlock the paid features without having to check the internet every time.

The app works normally with no connection. You can use it in airplane mode all day.

Children

Preço Certo is a professional tool for confectionery and bakery work and is not directed at children. Since it collects no data at all, it also collects no data from children.

Your rights

Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), and similar laws elsewhere, give you the right to access, correct and delete your personal data. With Preço Certo those rights are exercised on the device itself, because that is where everything is:

  • Access and correct: all content is visible and editable inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: deleting an item inside the app removes it permanently. Uninstalling the app erases everything at once.
  • Backup: if you use Android backup, remember to delete the copy in your Google Drive as well, through your device settings.

We hold no user database to query or erase on request. Even so, if you have any question about this, write to flrsistemas@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data in a new way, this page will be updated before that change goes live, and the effective date at the top will change. It is worth re-reading after a major update.
